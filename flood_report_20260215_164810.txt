  - Stations >1.0 indicate prolonged surface inundation and high soil saturation risk (example: (55.3944,10.4891), score 3.003). Expect oxygen depletion in topsoil within 24–72 hours.
  - Consequences: root hypoxia, reduced root respiration, root death in sensitive species.

  • Nutrient leaching and transformations
  - Nitrate leaching risk in sandy/structured soils where standing water drains away; in stagnant water, denitrification will convert NO3– to N2/N2O (net N loss).
